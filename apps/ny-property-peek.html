NY Property Peek

Web app Maps Open data

A map for looking up property details anywhere in New York State. Search an address or click the map to see year built, square footage, school district, and more.

Try it live (opens in a new tab)

The problem

New York's property records are split across 62 counties and 1,528 municipalities. Looking up basic facts about a house, like when it was built or which school district it's in, can mean hunting through dozens of separate county portals, each with its own interface.

What I built

One statewide map covering more than 5.5 million tax parcels from New York State's GIS records. No account, no install.

  • Search by address, or click anywhere on the map
  • See year built, square footage, school district, property class, assessed and market value, and acreage
  • Parcel boundaries drawn on the map for the 38 counties that publish them
  • Google Street View of the property
  • County pages that break down each county's housing stock

Data source

New York State's public GIS tax parcel records.

One interesting decision

Clicking the map looks up the property at that spot. But each of the 5.5 million parcels is stored as a single point near the middle of its lot, not its full outline. A plain "find the nearest point" search always returns something: click the middle of a lake or a large park, and you'd get a house half a mile away, shown as if it were the spot you clicked.

So the lookup only accepts a match within 75 meters (about 250 feet). Click a lot and you get that property; click empty space and you correctly get nothing.

It stays fast because of a spatial index, which organizes parcels by location the way a filing cabinet organizes folders by drawer. Instead of checking all 5.5 million parcels, the lookup opens only the drawer for the area around the click and compares the few properties inside, so results come back in milliseconds.
