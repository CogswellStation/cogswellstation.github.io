NY Property Peek

Web app Maps Open data

A map for looking up property details anywhere in New York State. Search an address or click the map to see year built, square footage, school district, and more.

Try it live

The problem

New York's property records are split across 62 counties and 1,528 municipalities. Looking up basic facts about a house, like when it was built or which school district it's in, can mean hunting through dozens of separate county portals, each with its own interface.

What I built

One statewide map covering more than 5.5 million tax parcels from New York State's GIS records. No account, no install.

  • Search by address, or click anywhere on the map
  • See year built, square footage, school district, property class, assessed and market value, and acreage
  • Parcel boundaries drawn on the map for the 38 counties that publish them
  • Google Street View of the property
  • County pages that break down each county's housing stock

Data source

New York State's public GIS tax parcel records.
