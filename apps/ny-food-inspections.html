NYFoodInspections

Web app Maps Open data

Current health inspection grades for restaurants across New York State, from official public data. Not reviews, and not opinions about the food, service, or ambience.

Try it live

The problem

Restaurant inspection results are public, but they're hard to use. They sit in health department records as lists of violations, and outside of places that post letter grades there's no simple answer to "how did this restaurant do?" Review sites mix food, service, and atmosphere into one score that says nothing about sanitation.

What I built

A statewide search and map built only on official inspection data.

  • Search restaurants by name or location, or browse the map
  • Tap a result or map pin to see its current grade
  • View each restaurant's inspection history

Data source

Official health department inspection and violation records.

One interesting decision

Not every health department issues letter grades. Where an official grade exists, the app shows it as-is. Where it doesn't, the app calculates one from the violation records using a model based on LA County's letter-grade system, so every restaurant can be compared on the same scale.
